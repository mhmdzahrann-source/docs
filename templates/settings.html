WhatsApp reservation bot

Answers customers on your WhatsApp Business number 24/7 (Arabic + English) and takes table bookings.

Needs the whatsapp_business_messaging permission. Leave blank to keep the saved token.

Meta for Developers → your app → WhatsApp → API Setup.

WhatsApp webhook

In Meta for Developers → WhatsApp → Configuration, set the callback URL to the address below and subscribe to the messages field:

https://YOUR_DOMAIN/webhook/whatsapp

It uses the same verify token and app secret as the Instagram webhook (from .env).
