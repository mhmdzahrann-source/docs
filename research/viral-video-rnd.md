# Viral short-form video R&D (Reels / TikTok / Shorts), November 2026

Goal: find the video **formats** that are getting reach right now, so we can
reuse their structure with our own content, editing them with the
`video-cut` and `audio-cleanup` skills.

> **"Clone the format, not the video."** Re-uploading or copying someone's
> actual video, voice, footage or script is copyright infringement, and
> Instagram/TikTok both detect and down-rank reposted content. Formats,
> structures, hook patterns, pacing and trends are free for everyone to use,
> and that is what every viral creator copies.

---

## 1. What the platforms reward right now

Most sources are creator and marketing blogs, not official platform data, so
treat exact numbers as rough. These points come up in most of them:

| Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Hook in the first 1-2 seconds** | Most scrolling decisions happen in about 1s. Reels should hook by 1s and promise the payoff by 3s. |
| **Watch time + rewatches** | Repeatedly named as the strongest signal on Reels. Replays count. |
| **Sends (DM shares) and saves** | Count more than likes. Sends drive reach to non-followers. |
| **Native, UGC feel** | Phone-shot, talking-to-camera beats polished ads. Heavy transitions and hard-sell hooks are losing ground. |
| **Adapt per platform** | Reposting the exact same cut everywhere reportedly loses retention. Change the hook and length per platform. |
| **Length** | Reels: 7-30s sweet spot. TikTok: longer (60s+) works if retention holds. |

**What this means for editing:** cut all dead air (`video-cut`), make the
voice clean and loud (`audio-cleanup`), put burned-in captions on (most
people watch muted), and start on the hook with no intro.

## 2. Formats getting reach (reuse these structures)

| # | Format | Structure | Why it spreads | Edit style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Comment-to-get** | "Comment **GUIDE** and I'll DM you…" → show 3 seconds of the value → CTA | Comments push reach, and DMs build a list. **Your Instagram tool automates this.** | Fast cuts, CTA text on screen whole video |
| 2 | **Looping checklist** | "5 apps I use every day" → quick list → last item flows into the first | Rewatches, saves | 0.25s jump cuts, numbered text |
| 3 | **POV skit** | "POV: you…" text + relatable scene | Sends ("this is so you") | Natural pace, trending audio |
| 4 | **Outcome-first** | Show the result first ("I made $X / before→after"), then how | Curiosity, holds to the end | Result in frame 1, then rewind |
| 5 | **One-question street interview** | Same question to many people, rapid fire | Comments arguing answers | Hard cut between each person |
| 6 | **Myth vs truth / "Stop doing X"** | Bold wrong claim → correction | Comments + saves | Talking head, captions, zoom punch-ins |
| 7 | **Beat-synced montage** | Clips cut exactly on the music beat | Rewatches | Cut on beats, 0.3-0.8s clips |
| 8 | **Green-screen react / explainer** | You in front of a screenshot/article/tweet | Fast to make, news-jacking | Captions, arrow/circle highlights |
| 9 | **Day-in-the-life / build-in-public** | Short clips of a process with timestamps | Followers, parasocial | 1-2s clips, voice-over |
| 10 | **Micro-tutorial** | "How to X in 15 seconds": step 1, 2, 3, done | Saves | Screen recording + face cam, zooms |

## 3. Hook formulas (first sentence, about 1 second)

- **Curiosity gap:** "Nobody tells you this about ___."
- **Outcome-first:** "This 20-second trick saved me 3 hours a week."
- **Callout:** "If you post on Instagram and get under 1k views, watch this."
- **Contrarian:** "Stop using hashtags. Here's why."
- **Challenge:** "I tried ___ for 7 days."
- **Visual hook:** start mid-action, with something moving or changing in frame 1.
- **Number + promise:** "3 free AI tools that edit your videos for you."

Rules: say it **and** show it as on-screen text. Don't overpromise, because
viewers punish clickbait with fast swipes. Add a mini-hook every 10-15s
("but the 3rd one is the best…").

## 4. Ideas built on what you already have

You have an Instagram **comment-to-DM automation** tool in this repo. The
strongest play is to pair format #1 with it:

1. **"Comment EDIT and I'll send you my free video-editing checklist".**
   A reel showing a messy raw clip, then the cleaned and cut version (before/after
   = outcome-first). The tool auto-DMs the checklist to everyone who comments.
2. **"I built a bot that replies to every Instagram comment"** (build-in-public).
   Screen-record the dashboard plus a phone receiving the DM. Hook: "This
   bot answered 500 comments while I slept."
3. **"Bad audio vs fixed in 1 click"** (micro-tutorial / before-after).
   Play 2 seconds of noisy audio, then the `audio-cleanup` version. Very shareable
   for creators.
4. **"3 dead-air cuts that doubled my watch time"** (myth vs truth,
   checklist). Use `video-cut` dry-run stats ("removed 38% of this video")
   as on-screen proof.
5. **Weekly trend-jack:** every week, take the top trending audio or format in
   your niche (see the research workflow below) and make your version within
   48 hours.

## 5. Weekly R&D routine (repeatable)

1. **Collect 10-20 viral examples** in your niche from the last 14 days:
   TikTok Creative Center (trending hashtags, songs, top ads), Instagram
   Reels tab search for your niche keyword, and YouTube Shorts search with
   the "This week" filter. Save links in a sheet.
2. **Break each one down** into hook (first sentence plus first frame), format
   (from the table above), length, cut pace (cuts per 10s), captions yes/no,
   audio (trending, original or voice-over), and CTA.
3. **Score** with views ÷ follower count. Over 3x means the format, not the
   account size, is doing the work.
4. **Pick the top 3 formats** and write your own scripts using the hook
   formulas.
5. **Film, then edit** with `audio-cleanup` → `video-cut` → vertical →
   captions.
6. **Post, then measure after 48h:** 3-second hold, average watch %, sends,
   saves. Keep what works and drop what doesn't.
