# Khayal morning brief

Keep it short and readable on a phone.

1. **Yesterday:** reach, engagement and follower change per channel (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) via Windsor.ai. Highlight the best post.
2. **Needs a reply:** comments or messages asking about prices, booking or lessons. Draft a suggested reply for each in the brand voice. Do not send them.
3. **Today:** posts scheduled or waiting for approval in Metricool.
4. **One action:** the single most useful thing to do today (e.g. "film a sunset Reel — sunset Reels got 3× reach last week").

Save to `marketing/reports/brief-YYYY-MM-DD.md` and show it.
