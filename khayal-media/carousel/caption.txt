We're proud to announce our partnership with Nafas and Havana, the partners behind our first endurance race. 🐎

One month of training. A 10 km test race with random teams, tracked on Equilab, with a 18 km/h limit. Zero penalties, and everyone finishing together. And we all passed.

Congratulations to our winning team, and thank you to every rider who joined.

The challenge gets bigger next time. Details this Thursday.

Haven't renewed yet? Contact us.
Want to join? Let us know 2 days before. No walk-ins on training day.
