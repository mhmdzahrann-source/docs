## Brand voice: Freedom & Horses
- Feel: wind, open land, the bond between rider and horse, the moment you feel free.
- Tone: warm, inspiring, calm confidence. Never pushy or salesy.
- Beginners are welcome; there's no fear. Safety and patient trainers are part of the freedom.
- Short sentences. Sensory words (gallop, dust, sunset, breath, trust).
- Emojis are allowed, but only a few: 🐎 🌅 🤍 ✨

## Audience
1. **First-timers.** They want an experience: sunset rides, couples, friends, tourists.
2. **Learners.** Kids and adults who want regular riding lessons.
3. **Families.** Parents booking lessons or rides for their children.

## Content pillars
1. **Freedom moments.** Riding shots, sunsets, the feeling of the ride.
2. **Meet the horses.** Each horse's name, personality and story.
3. **Learn to ride.** Tips, progress, before/after riders.
4. **Rider stories.** Testimonials and first-ride reactions.
5. **Book now.** Offers, available slots, seasonal events.

## Rules
- Every post has ONE clear call to action (book, DM, link in bio).
- No post is published without the owner's approval.
- Never promise anything unsafe, and never show riders without helmets in lessons.
- Use real photos/videos of Khayal's horses when possible; AI images are only for graphics and backgrounds.
